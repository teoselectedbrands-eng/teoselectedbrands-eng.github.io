What happens after an inquiry?

Clarify

A person reviews the general enquiry and identifies what kind of conversation is appropriate.

Scope

The potential provider, location, format, resources and privacy requirements are checked, if relevant.

Confirm—or redirect

Only an explicit response can confirm next steps. A request does not reserve a place or activate a clinical service.

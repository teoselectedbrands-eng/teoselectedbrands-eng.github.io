STEP 02 · THE COORDINATION LOOP

A real secretary is a process, not a chair.

Aetherion may use AI assistance behind the scenes to organise a draft, a checklist or a follow-up. A public email does not itself create an appointment; operational confirmation comes from the appropriate authorised coordinator.

REQUEST

1. Receive & clarify

Identify the topic, useful response route and the smallest non-sensitive information needed.

ROUTE

2. Determine who owns it

Learning, community, administrative coordination and regulated clinical questions follow separate boundaries.

PROPOSE

3. Explore a possible time

Check real people, place, accessibility, schedule and delivery mode. Never confuse a preference with availability.

CONFIRM

4. Send actual details

Only a verified slot becomes an appointment, invitation or event, with venue or private online joining instructions.

PREPARE

5. Share practical guidance

Time zone, access arrangements, what to bring and contact instructions are issued for the confirmed occurrence.

FOLLOW UP

6. Resolve & learn

Mark the enquiry answered or needing follow-up. Improve the route based on recurring friction without exposing private messages.

Current operating state: a real general email address is published and has been tested by the owner. The website does not yet provide a live public appointment inventory, dedicated AI chat agent, automated reply guarantee or clinical intake portal.

TRUTHFUL READINESS

What remains separate?

Does the AI secretary book and confirm times automatically?

No. AI assistance can help the owner prepare and organise correspondence, but this public static page does not autonomously access calendars or confirm bookings.

Can someone ask about occupational therapy or physiotherapy?

Yes, for the appropriate professional contact route only. No diagnosis, personal care records or confidential clinical intake belongs in the general email pathway.

Is a private workshop invitation public registration?

No. Organiser-held meeting links and invitations are sent only through the event's authorised confirmation process.

Where is the complete content index?

The Discover directory is Aetherion's existing wider catalogue of actual pages. It keeps News, Journal, learning, local discoveries and programme concepts distinguishable.

Choose a question · Free, independent learning

A small question can open a whole field.

Don't start with a catalogue. Start with something you can notice, compare or try on paper. These three entry paths lead to materials you can use today—no account, scoring or personal data collection.

A SIX-QUESTION GUIDE

Before choosing a class, ask…

  1. What draws me to this practice right now?
  2. What will actually happen in a session?
  3. Who teaches it, and what experience do they have?
  4. How can intensity or tasks be adapted?
  5. What equipment and environment does it require?
  6. What does the class offer—and what does it not claim to replace?

These are consumer and learning questions, not a fitness or medical eligibility assessment.

FOLLOW YOUR
CURIOSITY.

Begin with an ordinary action, a place or something you noticed. Aetherion’s learning tracks connect observation, movement culture, play and facilitation.

Two starting points.

The first two pilot courses have short introductory experiences and free worksheets to explore. Their complete lesson drafts remain under review. The other nineteen courses are curriculum outlines, rather than courses available for enrolment.

Initial design constraint

Proposed revision

A facilitator who listens to the room

Initial design constraint
Long instructions and public speaking requirements can limit voluntary participation.
Proposed revision
Offer building, sketching or observing, plan transitions, separate evidence from interpretations and document one justified revision.
Still unknown
How real participants would experience the session, whether choices were usable, and which outcomes could be supported by evidence.

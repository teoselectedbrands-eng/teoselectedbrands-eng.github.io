Choosing a Practice

A comparison sheet for an adult exploring a real provider. Keep unconfirmed answers blank. Aetherion currently offers learning materials; its class formats are concepts.

Purpose

What would I like to explore or learn?

Session

What actually happens, and how long does it last?

Facilitator

Who leads it? What relevant experience can the provider verify?

Choice

Can I pause, observe or ask for alternatives?

Access

What venue, equipment, travel and access arrangements are confirmed?

Terms

What are the real date, total cost, cancellation terms and confirmation process?

Decision

What fits my purpose? What still needs an answer before I commit?

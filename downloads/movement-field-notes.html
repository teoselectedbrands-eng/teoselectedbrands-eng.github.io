Movement Field Notes

A reflection sheet for an adult reader, learner or facilitator. Use a fictional scene or permitted footage; physical participation is optional.

Scene

Describe the action, task and setting. Avoid identifying information.

What I noticed

Record actions, directions, timing and spatial relationships. Leave motives and diagnoses out.

Possible interpretations

Write at least two explanations. What else might be happening?

Missing context

What happened before and after? What is outside the frame? Whose perspective is missing?

A comparison

Which difference would you like to inspect? What else changes at the same time?

Next question

Write one answerable question, and what you would need to answer it.

Limits

What cannot be concluded from these notes?

Educational reflection only; not assessment or individual treatment. No information is sent to Aetherion. Original Aetherion material: you may print and share unchanged with attribution for non-commercial learning.

See Before You Explain

A fictional person walks toward a doorway, stops, looks right and turns back. The actions can be described; the person’s reason is not supplied.

Four questions for your notes.

Use a fictional or non-identifying example. Write on your own paper, say your notes aloud or use a drawing. Nothing is submitted to Aetherion.

What could another observer verify?

Write only actions, positions or words given in the scene.

What might explain the action?

List at least two possibilities and label them as interpretations.

What is missing?

Identify information that could change your reading.

What is a respectful next question?

Ask openly, or decide that asking is unnecessary.

Educational reflection, not an assessment or individual exercise plan.

A Movement in Context

Imagine carrying a bag from a shop to a table. A second version adds stairs, a narrow doorway and a deadline. The object may be unchanged while the task’s conditions differ.

Four questions for your notes.

Use a fictional or non-identifying example. Write on your own paper, say your notes aloud or use a drawing. Nothing is submitted to Aetherion.

What task and goal?

Name the start, finish and purpose.

Which object and route?

Describe handles, surfaces, supports and turning points.

What about time and other people?

Include timing, shared space and agreed assistance when relevant.

Which one condition would you change?

Compare a modest alternative and record what remains unknown.

Educational reflection, not an assessment or individual exercise plan.

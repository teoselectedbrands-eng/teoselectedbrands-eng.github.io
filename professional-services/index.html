How an actual appointment could be arranged

Inquiry is not booking.

We currently have a published general contact email, but not a verified named secretary, staffed booking calendar or clinical intake portal. Coordination should proceed in three distinct steps.

1 · Request the right route

Ask for a professional contact without sending personal health histories through ordinary email.

2 · Verify the service

Confirm practitioner's scope, qualification, accessibility, privacy, available session and informed consent route.

3 · Confirm the appointment

Only the authorised coordinator provides an actual date, location, terms and a private meeting link if applicable.

Ask for professional coordination ↗Open the private-contact enquiry pathway ↗

Online educational calls may later use Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams after an invitation is confirmed. Clinical telehealth needs separate suitability and privacy arrangements. No public meeting link or automatic reservation is offered here.

Before the first encounter

Questions that matter.

Can I book directly on this site?

Not yet. No publicly bookable clinician calendar is active. A general enquiry can request the appropriate private contact route.

Does the movement catalogue offer therapy?

No. Public class concepts and professional occupational therapy or physiotherapy assessment have different responsibilities and must not be conflated.

Are sessions available online?

Online educational meetings may be arranged after an authorised invitation. Professional care requires clinical suitability, a consent process and a verified private channel. Do not share medical records through the public website.

What else can I explore?

See Aetherion News Edition 02 for independent evidence-led reporting, or Living & Inclusion for general accessibility concepts. Neither is individual medical advice.

UNEP puts climate adaptation needs at US$305 billion a year by 2035

Aetherion News · Source published 4 November 2026

In a report published on 4 November 2026, UNEP estimated that developing countries will need about US$305 billion a year by 2035 to adapt to climate impacts. It separately tracked US$28.2–31.7 billion in international public adaptation finance from developed to developing countries in 2024.

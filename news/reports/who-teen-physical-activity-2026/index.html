WHO estimates a persistent gap in teen physical activity

Aetherion News · Source published 4 November 2026

In an analysis released on 4 November 2026, WHO estimated that about one in four adolescents aged 11–17 reported zero days with at least 60 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity in the previous week. The 2025 estimate draws on 389 surveys across 140 countries and territories. The share was 28% for girls and 22% for boys. WHO reports that overall levels changed little since 2010.

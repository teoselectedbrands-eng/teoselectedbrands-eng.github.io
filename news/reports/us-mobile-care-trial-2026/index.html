US trial records fewer deaths in mobile-care group

Aetherion News · Source published 4 November 2026

A five-city US trial published on 4 November 2026 enrolled 409 adults with opioid use disorder who injected drugs. By 52 weeks, there were four deaths among 203 participants offered mobile care plus peer support, and 13 among 206 linked to community services through peer support. Deaths were a secondary outcome. The main measures tracked use of medicines for HIV prevention and opioid use disorder, with similar results across groups.

Open Research Europe widens access to fee-free publishing

Aetherion News · Source published 3 November 2026

Open Research Europe opened submissions on its relaunched platform on 2 November 2026, extending eligibility to researchers affiliated with institutions in 11 participating European countries. Eligible authors can publish free of charge, and readers can access the articles freely. CERN, the platform’s new operator, announced the relaunch on 3 November.

Hubble study traces oxygen in 37 nearby dwarf galaxies

Aetherion News · Source published 2 November 2026

NASA reported on 2 November 2026 that researchers used Hubble and other telescopes to estimate how much oxygen stars produced in 37 nearby dwarf galaxies and how much stayed there. The team found that the galaxies retained a small share. The researchers point to winds powered by massive stars and supernovae as a likely explanation. Where the oxygen ended up remains open.

Report finds a gap in classroom use of learning assessments

Aetherion News · Source published November 2026

Among 86 low- and middle-income countries surveyed in 2026, about seven in ten reported using learning assessment results nationwide to guide education policy and planning. About four in ten reported using the results to guide teaching across all schools. UNICEF and the Hempel Foundation published the findings in November 2026.

The survey ran from May to July and drew on responses from education ministries and UNICEF country offices. The findings describe practices reported at the country level. They leave open how consistently individual schools use the results and what effect this has on pupils’ learning.

The report highlights a role for school leaders and district offices in helping teachers interpret the results and identify where pupils need support.

Lebanon launches a disability inclusion strategy through 2030

Aetherion News · Source published 3 November 2026

Lebanon’s Ministry of Social Affairs launched the 2026–2030 disability inclusion strategy in Beirut on 3 November 2026. The plan sets priorities for education, employment and accessibility. It also calls for accessible evacuation, shelters and health services during emergencies.

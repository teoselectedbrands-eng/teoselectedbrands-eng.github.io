UN agencies report the scale of Afghanistan’s school access gap

Aetherion News · Source published 4 November 2026

UNESCO and UNICEF released a joint assessment on 4 November 2026 estimating that 53% of Afghanistan’s primary-school-age children were outside formal general education. The agencies also reported that around 5,300 community-based classes were operating by October, reaching about 162,000 learners. Girls made up 64% of learners in those classes.

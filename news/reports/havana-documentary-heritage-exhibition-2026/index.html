Havana exhibition showcases Cuba’s documentary heritage

Aetherion News · Source published 4 November 2026

An exhibition opened at UNESCO’s Havana office on 3 November 2026, bringing together images of historic documents and collections listed in the Memory of the World registers. Among the collections represented are José Martí’s documentary archive and the Cuban Film Posters Collection.

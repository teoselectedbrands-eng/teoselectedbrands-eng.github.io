Kadeena Cox sets two world records at the Para Cycling Track Worlds

Aetherion News · Championships 29 October–1 November 2026

At the Para Cycling Track World Championships in Apeldoorn, held from 29 October to 1 November 2026, Kadeena Cox won the women’s C4 kilometre time trial in a world-record 1:09.545. She set a second world record of 11.409 seconds in sprint qualifying and went on to win the sprint title.

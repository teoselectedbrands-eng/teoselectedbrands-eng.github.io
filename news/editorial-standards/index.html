NEWSROOM GOVERNANCE · OWNER REVIEW DRAFT

Our editorial standards.

Aetherion News is being prepared as a source-led publication. We will not label a demonstration, a creative scenario or an organizational announcement as verified News.

Original sources and verification

Each report must identify the event being reported, the Original sources supporting material claims, source dates, access dates, evidence limitations, editorial review and any important unresolved uncertainty. Primary records take priority when accessible. Every published factual claim must be traceable to a source and reviewed before publication.

Separate dates and interpretations

We distinguish the date an event occurred, when a source was published or updated, and when Aetherion published or modified its own report. Commentary and inference must be distinct from source-established facts.

Corrections and follow-ups

Corrections must remain visible, dated and appropriately described. Significant factual errors require an explicit record of what changed and why. A retractable report must preserve a transparent audit trail and not silently erase a known correction. Follow-up stories distinguish new developments from the original event.

News, Journal and Aetherion Updates

News reports verified time-sensitive developments. The Journal provides essays, longer-lasting interpretation and clearly labeled creative companions. Aetherion Updates are official organizational notices; they are not independent factual News reporting. HavenLine agency records and clinical information are separate and never a public news feed.

Publication status

No verified News reports have been published on this proposed destination. This policy page is a pre-deployment draft, subject to owner and editorial approval. It is not a certification of any external newsroom standard.

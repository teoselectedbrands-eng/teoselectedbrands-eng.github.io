Occupational Therapy, Rehabilitation & Functioning

Human functioning is about activity and participation in the contexts where people live, learn and work.

Professional practice and editorial information are separate. Aetherion Functioning Lab is an occupational therapy practice in Lebanon. This page introduces professional concepts and boundaries; it does not provide a clinical assessment, a confirmed appointment slot or medical advice. The website's general mailbox is not a clinical intake channel.

What do we mean by functioning?

Functioning asks what a person does, wants to do and needs to participate in—within real environments. A health condition may matter, but so do activities, participation, tools, relationships and the physical and social setting. The WHO International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health (ICF) provides a useful reference framework.

Occupational Therapy

Occupational therapy considers meaningful everyday activities such as self-care, work, school routines and community participation. Evaluation and intervention require individual professional judgement and appropriate consent.

Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation can involve restoring, maintaining or adapting functioning in relation to a condition or life change. Approaches vary by needs, setting and the professional disciplines involved.

Assistive Products & Environments

Devices, environmental changes and accessible design can influence participation. The choice of a product or adaptation depends on the individual and the context, not a universal recommendation.

Finding the appropriate route

  1. Reading or research: continue to the Journal or Learn library for public educational information.
  2. Professional scope: review the existing professional-services boundary before enquiring.
  3. Individual clinical care: use the practice's established professional coordination route; avoid sending health records to the editorial website mailbox.
  4. Urgent medical concern: use an appropriate local emergency or healthcare service, not the website.

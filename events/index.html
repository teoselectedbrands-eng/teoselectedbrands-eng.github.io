When an idea becomes a real encounter.

The programme

The question, activity sequence and intended learning remain the starting point. A programme can have more than one possible delivery format.

The occurrence

A confirmed listing will give its date, time zone, host, facilitator, venue or online format, access information, attendance terms and registration method before booking opens.

Completed encounters may lead to a reading list, a new activity card or an account of what the group explored. Identifying work or images would require appropriate permission.

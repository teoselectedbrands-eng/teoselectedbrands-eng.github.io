MOVEMENT TECHNOLOGY & MEASUREMENT

A sensor records signals, not the entire story.

A wearable may estimate movement or activity intensity; a clock measures duration; a map can represent a route. None alone establishes meaning, belonging, personal ability or clinical suitability.

WHAT WAS MEASURED?

Signal, unit, uncertainty

Ask which variable was recorded, with what device, calibration, validation and measurement error.

WHAT MATTERED TO THE PERSON?

Purpose, choice, participation

Ask which options existed, how access affected participation and whether an individual preferred the activity.

Five questions before trusting a movement number

What was actually detected? Who was studied? How accurate was the estimate? Which conditions might distort it? Was informed consent and privacy respected?

CONCEPT / ADULT MOVEMENT CULTURE · NOT SCHEDULED

The Movement Reading Room

An exploratory workshop for adults interested in sport, movement and learning. Participants look at a video or movement sequence, distinguish what can be observed from what is inferred, and discuss what the environment and task change.

ObservationMovement literacyDiscussion
Unfold a sample 4-part session

1. Notice: Participants describe a short movement scene using verbs, timings and spatial relationships.

2. Compare: Small groups look for differences in interpretations.

3. Contextualise: The group considers goals, equipment, rules and environment.

4. Reframe: Participants produce one better question they could investigate next.

Proposed educational format. No dates, pricing, facilitator assignment or registration have been confirmed.

CONCEPT / COMMUNITY FIELD NOTES · NOT SCHEDULED

Movement, Space & City

An outdoor or tabletop exploration of how places invite different physical practices—from running paths and ramps to public courts and informal meeting points.

Unfold the learning outcome

Participants create a small map of movement opportunities and barriers, marking observed features separately from personal interpretations or proposed changes.

Concept only; public events and visits require confirmed venues, permissions and facilitators.

SEPARATE LEARNING TRACK / SCHOOL TEAMS

Below the Surface: Observation to Responsible Action

An existing educational concept for school teams. This is not a children's fitness programme and does not turn the Aetherion public identity toward paediatric healthcare. Its curriculum and facilitator requirements remain a separate review stream.

What the concept explores

Observable events, context and sequence, plural hypotheses, missing perspectives and proportionate next information moves. A provisional 150-minute format is being developed; it is not advertised as an available booking.

A USEFUL COMPARISON

Find the practice behind the label.

Two classes with the same name may differ in pace, group size, teaching style, equipment and adaptation. Start with the actual class conditions, not an assumed promise of results.

Calisthenics Foundations

Explore pushing, pulling, supportive positions and progressions through bodyweight movement. A format study, not a live class.

Beginner20–30 min study formatSmall group or guided practice
What does this format involve?

Educational focus. Recognise how the equipment, available support and goal change the task.

Equipment. Mat; suitable bars only where supervised

Audience. Young adults / adults

Reading these formats. Durations and settings illustrate possible structures. Five are development concepts; Mobility Explorer is an editorial example. No sessions, instructors, dates or capacity are confirmed here.

A class is not a clinical assessment.

These formats describe general movement learning. If someone needs post-injury rehabilitation or an individual health assessment, that is a distinct professional pathway with its own suitability decision, practitioner responsibility and confidential processes.

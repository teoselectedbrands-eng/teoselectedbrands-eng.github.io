Practice method · Educational programme concept

Outdoor Movement & Living Environments

Walking, climbing preparation, carrying, reaching, ropes, route design and movement stations in a shared environment—not an outdoor gym built only around machines.

Not currently a bookable class

What this practice means

Movement outdoors can combine skill learning, exploration, nature, social interaction and physical challenge. The field matters: ground, weather, route, distance, materials, support, participants, instructors and opportunities to pause all change the activity.

Practitioner note: The proposed 800–900 m² outdoor field is a concept, not a surveyed or certified installation. Ropes, elevated structures, trees and suspended equipment must never be assumed safe without competent design and site-specific inspection.

Exercises, activities & materials

These are examples of what a qualified facilitator might develop into a programme. They are not complete instructions or a self-treatment plan.

Access

Arrival & accessible route

Explore the approach, mobility and access needs before adding stations or obstacles.

Ground

Walking and terrain variation

Compare surface textures and gradients; choose stable and accessible alternatives.

Support

Low-height reach and grip

Introduce appropriate handholds or objects; overhead load-bearing and structural ratings must be engineered.

Carry

Object transport

Use safe, light tools and team tasks for carrying, spatial planning and cooperation.

Cooperation

Group route design

Participants define a route, roles and rest points within an actual site.

Recover

Shade and reset

Plan water, weather response, rest, washrooms, emergency access and safe exits.

How learning may progress

Three educational phases—not guaranteed competency levels or a fixed number of sessions.

Site survey

Map actual boundaries, accessible circulation, sun, slopes and available human supervision.

Station pilot

Prototype low-risk, reversible materials with verified maintenance and capacity limits.

Program progression

Expand stations only after engineering, accessibility, insurance and facilitator review.

How sessions could be organised

Individual

1:1

Private teaching or assessment when suitable. Planned around one participant's aims.

Partner

1:2

Two participants with compatible goals; individual attention is still available.

Micro-group

1:3

Small facilitated format (maximum three participants), subject to service, privacy and setting suitability.

Group event

Separate programme

Larger group workshops, family sessions, outdoor events and demonstrations have their own facilitators, capacity and safety plan.

Availability, schedule, personnel and fees are not yet announced. Progression is based on readiness and goals, not an automatic move from 1:1 to larger groups.

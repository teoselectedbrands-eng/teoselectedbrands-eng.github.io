Beyond Playing Games: Discovering Lebanon’s Game-Making Community

Lebanon · Published 6 November 2026 · Source check: 6 November 2026

A video game is more than code. Behind interactive experiences are designers, artists, writers, musicians and people who enjoy creating worlds that others can explore.

Introducing LebGameDev

Lebanese Game Developers, or LebGameDev, describes itself as a community initiative supporting aspiring game makers and introducing game development to new audiences.

Its stated community spans programmers, artists, writers, animators, musicians, architects and people from other backgrounds. The process of building a game can involve visual design, storytelling, sound, interactions and testing.

Learning by making

Game jams are one common collaborative format in game-making circles: participants work together to create small games within a limited period. LebGameDev’s website preserves records of earlier community events, including coverage of Global Game Jam 2020.

Those archives help document game-making culture in Lebanon. They are not evidence that new jams or gatherings are currently scheduled.

What to explore next

If you are curious about game development, start by experimenting with a small idea, exploring simple interactive tools or learning how a game combines rules, visuals and feedback. Check directly with the community for current contact and activity details.

Source dates and limitations

Historical community profile based on the project’s own site, not a claim of active events in November 2026. Current operational status has not been independently confirmed. Checked 6 November 2026.

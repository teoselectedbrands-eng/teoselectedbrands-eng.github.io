Schools Without Walls: Learning From Nature in Lebanon

Editorial publication: 6 November 2026 · Sources checked: 6 November 2026 · SPNL program discussion: July 2026

What changes when a lesson moves out of the classroom and into a local habitat? Field learning can place observation and environmental responsibility in the same experience.

What the source establishes

In a July 2026 report about a meeting with Lebanon's Ministry of Youth and Sports, SPNL discussed Schools Without Walls, Hima Guardians and Hima Trails as approaches to youth involvement in community-based conservation.

Why it is worth discovering

The opportunity here is to connect environmental education with place-based learning: observing living systems, recording questions and participating in local stewardship.

What to explore or verify next

This report concerns programs discussed by SPNL, not an announcement that all schools can join an active public schedule. Interested educators should contact the organisation for eligibility and verified opportunities.

Source dates and limitations

Single organisational source describing partnership discussions and program intentions. It does not demonstrate broad national implementation or outcomes.

The source was accessed for this article on 6 November 2026. Event and program dates are not the article publication date.

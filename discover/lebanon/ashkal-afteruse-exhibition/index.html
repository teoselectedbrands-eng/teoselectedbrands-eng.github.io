Afteruse: When Everyday Objects Become Art

Editorial publication: 6 November 2026 · Sources checked: 6 November 2026 · 16 October 2026 – 15 January 2027

What remains after a familiar object has exhausted its first purpose? Artist Ali Merhi explores that question by working with materials that have already passed through daily life.

Announced dates

16 October 2026 – 15 January 2027. Verified against the organiser's published information on 6 November 2026; check the organiser for any changes.

What the source establishes

Ashkal Alwan lists Afteruse from 16 October 2026 to 15 January 2027. The exhibition draws on collected items such as food cans, containers, furniture and fragments, with motorised forms that move and produce sound.

Why it is worth discovering

Repurposed materials invite us to see repair, informal economies and the hidden histories of consumption. Instead of asking whether a used object is valuable, the exhibition can prompt a different question: what stories does its continued use carry?

What to explore or verify next

If you plan a visit, check the listed days and hours directly. If you are reading from afar, consider an everyday object whose function changed over time and what that transformation says about design.

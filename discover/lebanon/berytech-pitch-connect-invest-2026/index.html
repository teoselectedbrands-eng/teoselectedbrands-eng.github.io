Pitch. Connect. Invest.: Understanding an Innovation Event

Editorial publication: 6 November 2026 · Sources checked: 6 November 2026 · 16 November 2026 · 4:00–7:30 PM

A startup event can bring investors and founders into the same room, but the value for a visitor depends on which part of the ecosystem they are hoping to understand.

What the source establishes

Berytech has announced Pitch. Connect. Invest. for 16 November 2026 at Random Venue on the Beirut Waterfront, opening Global Entrepreneurship Week in Lebanon. Its published format includes startup demonstrations, conversations with investors, an open pitching station and networking.

Why it is worth discovering

The event offers a window into how new businesses present ideas, receive feedback and meet possible collaborators. Observation can be useful even for someone not currently building a company.

What to explore or verify next

Check registration requirements and participation criteria through Berytech's official page. A planned opportunity to network does not guarantee investment, mentorship or admission.

Source dates and limitations

This is a preview of an announced event and makes no claim of attendance, confirmed investment outcomes or remaining ticket availability.

The source was accessed for this article on 6 November 2026. Event and program dates are not the article publication date.

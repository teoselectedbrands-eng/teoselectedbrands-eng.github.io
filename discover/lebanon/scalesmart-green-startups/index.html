ScaleSmart: What Does It Mean for a Startup to Be Investment-Ready?

Editorial publication: 6 November 2026 · Sources checked: 6 November 2026 · Application status: verify with organiser

Pitching can be the most visible part of entrepreneurship, but investment readiness usually involves less visible questions about financial models, governance and how a business grows.

What the source establishes

Berytech describes ScaleSmart as a structured program for scaling AgriFoodTech and CleanTech startups and SMEs. Its model includes four months of focused support and eight months of follow-up, covering financial modelling, investor materials and growth planning.

Why it is worth discovering

The program helps explain a distinction that matters well beyond startups: a compelling idea is not the same as a validated operation or a sustainable business model.

What to explore or verify next

Berytech's homepage and detailed program page display differing application messages. The detailed page currently says applications are closed while an expression of interest may be possible. Always verify the live application route and eligibility.

Source dates and limitations

This is an explanation of the program model, not a promise of funding, current recruitment or participant outcomes.

The source was accessed for this article on 6 November 2026. Event and program dates are not the article publication date.

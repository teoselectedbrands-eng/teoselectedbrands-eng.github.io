Fall for Print: How Independent Publishing Meets Art in Beirut

Editorial publication: 6 November 2026 · Sources checked: 6 November 2026 · 20–22 November 2026

A book fair can be more than a place to purchase books. At Beirut Art Center, Fall for Print brings artists, independent publishers, printers and collectives together around the printed page.

Announced dates

20–22 November 2026. Verified against the organiser's published information on 6 November 2026; check the organiser for any changes.

Calendar-based date status only. It does not confirm opening hours, tickets, eligibility or cancellation.

What the source establishes

The second edition is scheduled for 20–22 November 2026. The organiser describes a program involving books, zines, printmaking, risograph collectives, archives and a special photographic newspaper developed with Wide Open Newspapers.

Why it is worth discovering

Printing is a way to make ideas visible, circulate creative work and preserve things that might disappear online. A zine can be a small cultural record; an art book can be an exhibition that travels beyond the gallery.

What to explore or verify next

Readers interested in illustration, design, photography, writing or independent media can explore the announced participants and ask which activities are open to the public. Attendance conditions and exact sessions should be checked through the event page.

Source dates and limitations

This is a sourced event preview, not a report from attendance. Dates and program may change.

The source was accessed for this article on 6 November 2026. Event and program dates are not the article publication date.

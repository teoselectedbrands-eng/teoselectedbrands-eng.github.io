Becoming Icon: Discovering a Museum Collection in Beirut

Editorial publication: 6 November 2026 · Sources checked: 6 November 2026 · 17 July 2025 – 20 June 2027

A museum collection is not a fixed story. The works are collected, interpreted and displayed through choices that influence how visitors understand a culture.

Announced dates

17 July 2025 – 20 June 2027. Verified against the organiser's published information on 6 November 2026; check the organiser for any changes.

Calendar-based date status only. It does not confirm opening hours, tickets, eligibility or cancellation.

What the source establishes

Sursock Museum presents Becoming Icon, curated by Yasmine Chemali, in its collection galleries. The official exhibition listing gives a long exhibition period from 17 July 2025 to 20 June 2027.

Why it is worth discovering

Instead of treating a collection as a single final account of art history, look at what is made prominent, what forms of expression appear together and how the exhibition title shapes interpretation.

What to explore or verify next

Visitors can begin with one work, read the museum's description and ask whose perspective the collection represents. Current admission, accessibility and opening hours should be confirmed with the museum.

Source dates and limitations

This is an introductory guide based on the museum's own listing, not a newly opened show or an independently attended review.

The source was accessed for this article on 6 November 2026. Event and program dates are not the article publication date.

USEK's Asher Center: From a First Idea to a Tested Venture

Editorial publication: 6 November 2026 · Sources checked: 6 November 2026 · Evergreen program profile

Entrepreneurial education begins before a company exists. The first task may be learning how to test assumptions and understand whether a problem has a workable solution.

What the source establishes

The Asher Center for Innovation and Entrepreneurship at USEK describes programs involving validation sprints, incubation, competitions, mentorship and startup support. It says its purpose is to help transform ideas into ventures.

Why it is worth discovering

The center provides an example of how universities can connect teaching, practical experimentation and opportunities outside a conventional course sequence.

What to explore or verify next

Prospective applicants should review each program separately, since a centre's existence does not establish that a particular cohort is currently open or that a grant is guaranteed.

Source dates and limitations

Institutional profile from USEK's own descriptions. The programs and any claimed outcomes have not been independently audited.

The source was accessed for this article on 6 November 2026. Event and program dates are not the article publication date.

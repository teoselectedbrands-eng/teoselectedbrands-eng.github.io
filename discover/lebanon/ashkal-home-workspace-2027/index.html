Ashkal Alwan's Home Workspace Program: A Route Into Art Practice

Editorial publication: 6 November 2026 · Sources checked: 6 November 2026 · Application deadline: 20 November 2026

For artists who want time, conversation and sustained study, a long-form program can offer a different route from a single workshop.

Announced dates

Application deadline: 20 November 2026. Verified against the organiser's published information on 6 November 2026; check the organiser for any changes.

Calendar-based date status only. It does not confirm opening hours, tickets, eligibility or cancellation.

What the source establishes

Ashkal Alwan's Home Workspace Program has an open call for its 2027 edition, scheduled for 11 January–23 July 2027. It is described as tuition-free and primarily in English, with a shared studio, monthly stipend and up to eight selected fellows. The organisation lists 20 November 2026 as the deadline.

Why it is worth discovering

An artist's development depends not only on making objects, but also on peer criticism, research, mentorship and the freedom to test ideas. This is a useful example of how learning and professional practice can overlap.

Look more closely: Read an open call like a project brief

The Home Workspace Program's 2027 call names more than an attractive study opportunity. It describes a tuition-free program primarily in English, up to eight fellows, a shared studio and a stipend. The required time commitment and working language matter just as much as the benefits when deciding whether the experience fits.

The original call asks applicants for a one-page CV, a portfolio under 10 MB and a motivation letter of 300–500 words. These constraints encourage applicants to express what their practice investigates and why this particular learning environment matters. The announced 20 November 2026 deadline must still be checked with the organiser; a date-based notice is not a live application-status check.

Try these questions

  1. Verify the eligibility and full-time commitment in the original call.
  2. Check portfolio size, CV length and motivation-letter word limit.
  3. Separate a stated selection criterion from an assumption about your chances.

What happens after a date passes?

We retain useful reporting as an archive. The page tells readers when the original event or application deadline has passed and points them to the original source for updated arrangements. An expired announcement is not deleted or silently changed into a new edition.

The exact operating status of an event, admissions or application availability requires an organiser check. Read our editorial freshness policy.

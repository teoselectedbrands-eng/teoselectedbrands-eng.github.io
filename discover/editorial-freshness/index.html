EDITORIAL TRANSPARENCY

Dates, updates
and corrections.

A source-linked article should remain useful even when an event finishes, an application deadline passes or the original organisation changes its information.

Policy edition: 6 November 2026 · This policy applies to Aetherion Discover editorial material, not to third-party organisers.

1. What we verify

We prioritise primary sources such as an official museum program, university calendar, organising association or institution. We separate the date an event is scheduled, the date a source was checked, and the date our article was published. An organiser's announcement is not an independent attendance or outcome report.

2. What date labels mean

Scheduled for a later date means the announced calendar date has not arrived, not that tickets are available. Within the announced period means today's Beirut calendar date falls between published start and end dates; it does not guarantee the venue is open. Original date passed marks archival context, not proof of cancellation or completion. A deadline reaching its date never guarantees that applications are still accepted.

3. How articles age

Dated stories remain as historical records where they still have context and original sources. We do not silently replace a 2026 event with a 2027 edition. If a significant factual correction is needed, the page should show the correction and its date. If the event was rescheduled or canceled, we update only after obtaining reliable evidence from the organiser.

4. What is not a real-time check

Some pages show a date-based label calculated by the reader's browser using the calendar date in Asia/Beirut. This label changes according to the announced calendar dates. It does not contact organisers, confirm attendance, check availability, verify safety or detect cancellation. If scripting is unavailable, a neutral source-check statement remains visible.

5. How we handle claims

News is a separate destination for dated factual reports; Journal includes essays and explicitly labeled fictional companions; Learn offers resources and introductions. Discover guides may link to those destinations, but a cross-link does not turn an exhibition preview into a news investigation or a learning outline into an available course. No paid placement or partnership is implied by inclusion.

6. Review and correction pathway

Aetherion aims to check time-sensitive information ahead of key dates and when a substantive correction is reported. A stated target is not a guarantee of continuous monitoring or automatic updates. Please send corrections with a relevant original source, the affected page URL and what needs reviewing to hello@aetherionmovement.com.

For News-specific standards, see Aetherion News Editorial Standards.

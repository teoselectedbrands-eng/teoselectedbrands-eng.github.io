The Joy of Becoming: An Exhibition About Worlds That Keep Changing

Beirut · Published 6 November 2026 · Source check: 6 November 2026

What happens when an artwork is not simply an object to look at, but part of a world that seems to keep changing? Mohamad Kanaan’s The Joy of Becoming opens at Beirut Art Center on 12 November 2026.

Announced dates

12 November 2026 – 12 February 2027. Verified against the organiser's published information on 6 November 2026; check the organiser for any changes.

Calendar-based date status only. It does not confirm opening hours, tickets, eligibility or cancellation.

Worlds in formation

The exhibition builds on Kanaan’s long engagement with Zakariyya al-Qazwīnī’s thirteenth-century cosmography, ʿAjāʾib al-makhlūqāt wa-gharāʾib al-mawjūdāt. It brings together drawing, sculpture, architecture, textiles, moving image, sound and performance.

The project explores transformation, imagination and the possibility that the unfamiliar can become a space for curiosity.

Different ways to enter the exhibition

Visitors interested in art may start with the visual language; those interested in history may explore the medieval work behind it. Someone drawn to creative learning may be interested in experimentation, play and collaboration.

The artist’s work has also involved collaboration with children, but that does not mean every exhibition activity is suitable for every age. Ask the center about specific programs and accessibility arrangements.

Dates and program

Beirut Art Center lists the exhibition from 12 November 2026 to 12 February 2027 and anticipates performances, workshops and collective encounters during its run. Separate session details should be checked as they are released.

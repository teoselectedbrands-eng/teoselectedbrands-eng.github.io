What a free member account could add.

A future verified account could give access to expanded lessons and resources, optional lesson progress and account controls. Email-only verification is the preferred starting point. A newsletter would be a separate, optional choice that does not control access.

Registration is not open. There is no active member dashboard, sign-in, saved progress or personal-data collection on this site. The introductory material above works now without those features.

A small, clear account boundary.

Before accounts are enabled, the actual service must provide verified sign-in, secure sessions, server-side checks for private material, clear data-handling information and working access, export and deletion controls. Education accounts will be kept separate from clinical information. Child profiles and reflective lesson answers are outside the proposed initial account design.

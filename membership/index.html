What will make a learning pathway different from an article?

Open introduction

Read the learning question, first sample activities, a small PDF preview where one is actually published, and clear expectations.

Guided curriculum

Follow sequenced modules with objectives, supported practice, appropriate instructional materials and understanding checks.

Verified completion

An identified authorised instructor reviews the evidence of learning and confirms the applicable certificate type and record.

Certificates are not all equivalent. Aetherion may eventually issue its own participation, completion or achievement certificates with valid authorisation and responsible signatures. This does not mean accredited university credit or recognised professional CE/CPD. Those require separate external authority.

Where a course includes time-based participation or correct-answer requirements, the rules need server-side validation, clear accommodations, alternatives to invasive monitoring and a documented instructor override process. No automatic certificate issuance is active today.

A member account should have a purpose.

Future free membership is intended to preserve selected resources and individual learning progress, not to lock every article behind sign-up. No live account registration or database collection is enabled at this stage.

Minimal profile

Only necessary contact and access data, with email verification, account deletion and a clear retention policy.

Learning record

Track authorised participation, progress, accommodations only where necessary, and certificate evidence securely.

Programme enquiries

Keep a request separate from a real reservation. Only authorised humans and confirmed schedules create bookings.

How bookings and payment could work.

Online · future

Online workshop / Zoom

Publish a real event, available capacity and facilitator. Send access instructions only after confirmed registration and appropriate privacy consent. No fabricated meeting links.

In person · future

On-site workshop

Display whether a programme is open or proposed. If a fee is charged in person, explain it before enrolment; collect no online payment details.

Free introduction does not imply that every workshop is free. Programmes can have different participation terms, but no fees, currencies, schedules or active registrations are published here without approval.

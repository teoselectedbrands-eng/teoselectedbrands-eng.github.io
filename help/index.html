Frequently asked questions

20 answers

01. What is Aetherion?
Aetherion

Aetherion connects movement, open learning, original journalism, cultural exploration and community ideas. Its professional occupational therapy pathway is distinct from the public editorial and learning platform.

02. What can I explore right now?
Aetherion

You can read available Journal articles and News reports, use open learning resources and explore Discover, Movement and existing activity descriptions. Not every proposed class, course or workshop has a scheduled occurrence.

03. Can I book a Pilates, calisthenics or dance class?
Movement

These practices appear in our activity catalogue. A public timetable or instant booking is not currently confirmed. You may send a general availability enquiry; a listing is not proof that a session is running.

04. Do I need previous exercise experience?
Movement

The activity pages describe different learning aims and experience levels. Any real session should confirm the format, instructor, access needs and appropriate modifications before participation.

05. Are workshops in person or online?
Workshops

The workshop catalogue lists in-person topics for enquiry. A session can be confirmed once a facilitator, location, language and date are agreed. Open online learning is available separately.

06. Can a school or organisation request a custom workshop?
Workshops

Email us the topic, general audience, setting and learning goal. We discuss whether and how a workshop can be arranged for your group.

07. Are workshop dates or prices published?
Workshops

No standing workshop timetable or public price list is currently confirmed. Specific arrangements and costs, where applicable, are discussed privately before an agreement.

08. Can activities be adapted for different participants?
Workshops

The planning conversation can consider accessibility, age range, group size and participation options. Any requested adaptations are reviewed for a particular activity and setting; they are not guaranteed in advance.

09. Does Aetherion provide occupational therapy?
Clinical

Aetherion Functioning Lab has a distinct professional occupational therapy pathway. Educational articles on this site are not individual assessments, diagnoses or clinical treatment plans.

10. How do I ask about a clinical service?
Clinical

Choose Professional / Clinical Pathway in the optional email topics above to ask for the appropriate private contact route. Keep diagnoses, medical reports, case histories and identifying information about children out of your message.

11. What is the Living section for?
Living

The Living section explores ageing, disability, accessibility and participation in everyday environments. For questions about a specific professional service, ask for the appropriate private contact route.

12. Is learning free?
Learning

Current open learning articles, introductory materials and available downloads can be used without registration. Any future paid or restricted offerings will be described separately and clearly.

13. Can I create an account and save my progress?
Learning

Not yet. Member accounts, favourites, progress tracking and sequential lesson unlocking are planned features, not currently active on the public website.

14. Will I receive a certificate?
Learning

Not automatically. Publication of a resource, lesson or workshop concept does not imply certification or accredited continuing education.

15. May I use your worksheets in a class or group?
Learning

Check the reuse notice on each resource. Where sharing is permitted, keep the attribution and context. Contact us about adaptations, commercial reuse or uncertain permissions.

16. How are News, Journal and Discover different?
Journal & News

News reports dated developments and sources. Journal offers deeper editorial articles and clearly labelled creative companions. Discover connects readers with relevant places, ideas, reports and resources.

17. Can I suggest a story, researcher or interview?
Journal & News

Yes. Send a public source or a brief suggested subject. Suggestions are reviewed editorially and do not guarantee coverage, endorsement, publication or an interview.

18. Can I submit an event or community initiative?
Community

Yes. Share the organiser, official public link, confirmed date, location and a short description. We verify basic details, distinguish editorial coverage from sponsorship and decide independently whether to publish.

19. What information should an enquiry include?
Contact

Write a brief question and include the organisation or general audience if relevant. A topic is optional. Share only the information needed for a first reply.

20. How can I report a correction or accessibility problem?
Contact

Email us the page URL and a brief description. For a correction, use “Correction” in the subject. For an accessibility issue, you can choose “Accessibility & Privacy” above.

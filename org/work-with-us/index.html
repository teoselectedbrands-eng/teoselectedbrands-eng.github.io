Events · Not a booking calendar

From a good question to a real gathering.

A proposed event only becomes a public event when its host, facilitator, place or platform, access arrangements, date and attendance terms have been confirmed.

  1. Discover

    Choose an idea from Events or Workshops, or bring your own.

  2. Scope

    Describe who it serves, what it explores and the proposed format.

  3. Agree

    Record responsibilities, fee or cost-sharing arrangement, logistics, privacy and safeguarding.

  4. Confirm

    Only after approval, publish accurate attendance and registration details.

Commercial clarity

Working together should be clear for both sides.

01

Purpose & deliverables

A short written brief identifies the learning goal, audience, format, outputs and how we would assess completion.

02

Roles & resources

Identify the organiser, facilitator, venue or platform, materials, accessibility needs and any permissions.

03

Fees & decision

Where work is commissioned, confirm the quote, costs, cancellation terms and responsibilities in writing before delivering it.

No fixed fee, approved partnership, available practitioner or upcoming delivery is implied. Clinical assessments and treatment requests use a separate professional pathway: Professional services.

Next action · A short introductory email

Two lines can start the conversation.

Tell us what kind of organisation or project this is, what you want to explore, and whether you are thinking of an online or in-person experience. No long application or account required.

Example:

“We are a school team of around 15 educators. Could we explore a practical workshop on how room layout influences participation?”

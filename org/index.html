WORK WITH US / ORGANISATIONS AND PROFESSIONAL TEAMS

Something more than a volunteer invitation.

A school, company, professional practice or cultural organisation can explore a commissioned workshop, a co-designed event or a scoped knowledge project. Clear responsibilities, deliverables, fees where applicable, and an agreement come before delivery. This is separate from an individual choosing to participate in the community.

HUMAN COLLABORATION / IN DEVELOPMENT

A contribution can start anywhere.

We are developing a way for people to contribute remotely, prepare through shared learning, and shape local workshops, gatherings and social initiatives. Each role begins with a clear purpose and an agreed scope.

  1. Bring a skill or question

    Research, teaching, facilitation, design, documentation or local knowledge.

  2. Learn and prepare

    Connect relevant learning with the people, access needs and resources involved.

  3. Shape an encounter

    Agree the host, responsibilities, place, timing and participation terms before announcing a session.

  4. Reflect and improve

    Review what happened and use permission-based feedback to shape the next experience.

This pathway is in preparation. No recruitment, placements or Aetherion event bookings are open here.

Food Systems & Sustainability

Follow a food item from production to everyday decisions.

Related workshop topic: Food Systems & Sustainability · Sources checked: 6 November 2026 · Original educational resource

One idea to carry

Food may be lost or wasted at several stages: production, storage, transport, retail and households. Exploring a single item can reveal different causes and possible responses.

Try a fictional scenario

A fictional school canteen notices that some fruit is bruised during delivery and that untouched portions are left after lunch. One proposal is to tell everyone to “try harder”, without examining the system.

Use this three-step activity

  1. Draw the food journey from producer to plate.
  2. Place possible loss or waste points on the map, distinguishing observed information from guesses.
  3. Choose one question to investigate—for example about storage, portion planning or communication—before recommending a change.

Questions worth keeping

  • What could you learn by asking the people involved at different stages?
  • Which decisions can a school influence, and which depend on suppliers or policy?

Evidence and limits

FAO’s 2026 Food Loss and Waste Challenge Badge provides education on food journeys and activities for different age groups; this map activity is independent and original.

This is a learning activity, not a food safety inspection, nutritional prescription or promise of measured waste reduction.
